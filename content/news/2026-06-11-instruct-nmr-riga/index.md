From 8 to 11 June, Kirill was one of the tutors at the Instruct-ERIC course and
MR LATVIA workshop
[Modern NMR for Biomolecular Interactions: From Solution and Solid-State to Hyperpolarisation and AI](https://www.researchlatvia.gov.lv/en/latvia-hosts-first-ever-instruct-eric-international-training-course-biomolecular-nmr),
held at the Latvian Institute of Organic Synthesis in Riga. It was the first
Instruct-ERIC course hosted in Latvia, and brought together 13 PhD students,
postdocs and early-career researchers from 8 countries.

Kirill lectured on machine learning for molecules, from how molecular
structure is represented in machine learning models to their use alongside
NMR data in the study of biomolecules.
