Rubén attended the first edition of the workshop
[General-Purpose Machine-Learned Force Fields in Theory and Practice](https://gpmlffworkshop.github.io/),
held at the University of Luxembourg from 13 to 15 July. Over three days of
lectures, tutorials and hands-on sessions, the workshop brought together
researchers developing and applying general-purpose machine-learned force
fields for chemistry and materials science.

Rubén presented his poster *Accelerating convergence of ML/MM simulations with
mean field approach*, on how the EMLE scheme lets the MM environment relax
around a fixed ML region to obtain free energy profiles at a fraction of the
cost of conventional ML/MM simulations.
