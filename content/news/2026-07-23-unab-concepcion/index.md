From 20 to 23 July, Kirill visited the Department of Chemical Sciences of
[Universidad Andrés Bello](https://noticias.unab.cl/eminencia-de-la-quimica-computacional-realizo-inedita-serie-de-charlas-y-talleres-en-la-sede-concepcion-de-la-unab/)
(UNAB) in Concepción, Chile, at the invitation of Dr. María Fernanda Mendoza.

Over four days, graduate students and researchers from UNAB
and other universities in the region learned about enhanced sampling, the
Adaptive String Method and machine learning potentials for simulating enzyme
reactions, through lectures, hands-on sessions and individual meetings focused
on their own research projects.

The activity was funded by the Fondecyt de Iniciación project 11241225 led by
Dr. Mendoza, and strengthens the collaboration between UNAB and the
Universitat de València, including future exchanges of graduate students.
